=== SEARCH: O(n) vs O(log n) ===

demo (16 sorted values): 0 3 6 9 12 15 18 21 24 27 30 33 36 39 42 45
binary search 27 -> index 9 in 3 steps
binary search 28 -> not found (4 steps)

--- worst case: searching the LAST element ---
        n   linear steps   binary steps
     1024           1024             11
    65536          65536             17
  1048576        1048576             21

binary halves the range each step: ~log2(n) steps instead of n.
